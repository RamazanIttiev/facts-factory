# Everyday things, explained through one true story

Format: take something the viewer touches every day and explain how it works through the real story of the person who made it (or broke it). The explanation sits inside the plot. Category `everyday` in `plan/taxonomy.json`; entries in `plan/topics.json` (ids 28-42, status `todo`, sources `to_fetch`).

Every spine still has to go through `npm run facts`: the names, dates and numbers below are from memory and are not verified yet.
